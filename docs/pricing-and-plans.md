# Pricing and Plans

RetrieveIt.AI offers three subscription tiers with per-seat pricing. All plans are billed monthly.

## Subscription Tiers

| | Standard | Researcher | Deep Researcher |
|---|----------|------------|-----------------|
| **Price** | $30/seat/month | $75/seat/month | $250/seat/month |
| **Standard Queries** | 800/seat | 2,000/seat | 6,000/seat |
| **Advanced Queries** | 80/seat | 200/seat | 600/seat |
| **Storage** | 10 GB/seat | 50 GB/seat | 100 GB/seat |

### What's a "Seat"?

A seat is a member in your organization. Your monthly limits scale with your team size. For example, a 5-person team on the Standard plan gets:
- 4,000 standard queries (800 x 5)
- 400 advanced queries (80 x 5)
- 50 GB storage (10 GB x 5)

These limits are shared across the entire organization, not per-person.

### Standard vs Advanced Queries

- **Standard queries** use fast, efficient AI models for quick document search and straightforward questions
- **Advanced queries** use more powerful AI models for complex reasoning, multi-step analysis, and deeper research

You choose Standard or Advanced mode when chatting.

## Query Packs

If you need more queries beyond your monthly allocation, you can purchase query packs. Query pack balances persist across months until used.

| Pack | Price | Standard Queries | Advanced Queries |
|------|-------|------------------|------------------|
| Standard Pack | $20 | 500 | - |
| Advanced Pack | $50 | - | 200 |
| Mixed Pack | $30 | 500 | 50 |

Query packs are consumed after your monthly seat allocation is exhausted.

## How to Subscribe

1. Sign in to RetrieveIt.AI
2. Go to **Dashboard** > **Billing**
3. Select your plan tier
4. Enter your seat count
5. Complete checkout via Stripe

## Managing Your Subscription

From the **Billing** page you can:
- Change your plan tier
- Update seat count
- Update payment method
- View invoices
- Cancel your subscription

Click **Manage Subscription** to access the Stripe customer portal for these options.

## What Happens Without a Subscription?

You can sign in and browse the dashboard, but you cannot:
- Create workspaces
- Invite team members
- Upload documents
- Use chat

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I try it before subscribing?
Contact us for a demo or trial access.

### What happens if I hit my query limit?
You'll see a message that you've reached your monthly limit. You can purchase a query pack for immediate additional queries, or wait until the next billing cycle when limits reset.

### Can I change plans mid-month?
Yes. Plan changes take effect immediately. You'll be prorated for the remainder of the billing period.

### What happens if I remove a team member?
Your seat count decreases and your next invoice reflects the reduced seats. Monthly limits are recalculated based on the new seat count.
